## The Interactive Loop Now Works

```
Before meeting: open prep card → see where things stand → bring questions
     ↓
During meeting: have better conversation because you remember
     ↓
After meeting: 2-min voice dump → send to @paul
     ↓
@paul processes: 1-on-1 note + profile update + action items + prep card update + pulse update
     ↓
Next meeting: prep card is richer because last meeting was processed
     ↓
Repeat — every cycle makes the profiles deeper and the prep cards more useful
```

# Team Brain — Your 2nd Brain for Managing Michael & Sophia

> "The gap between a great 1-on-1 and a forgotten one is documentation. This vault closes that gap."

---

## How It Works

1. **After each 1-on-1**, record a 2-minute voice dump (phone memo, Windows Voice Recorder, or any app)
2. **Send the audio to Paul** (drag into chat, or save to `Recordings/Audio/` and tell Paul)
3. **Paul transcribes and structures** it into a proper 1-on-1 note using your templates
4. **Paul updates** the team member's profile with wins, trends, and follow-ups
5. **Paul extracts action items** and adds them to your task list

Your Google Meet recordings with Gemini transcription can also be dropped in here — Paul will incorporate them alongside your voice dumps.

## Your Daily Rhythm

### Before a 1-on-1
Open the team member's profile. Scroll to **Last Check-In** for a Suggested Question. Hit record.

### After a 1-on-1 (2 minutes)
Dump your brain: *"Just met with Michael. He's excited about X but blocked on Y. I promised to look into Z by Friday. His energy was solid."*

Send it to Paul. Done.

### Weekly Review (10 minutes)
Open [[Master Dashboard]]. Check:
- What action items are aging?
- Whose energy trend is dipping?
- What wins did I miss celebrating?

## Recorded Meetings (Google Meet + Gemini)

Your Google Meet recordings with Gemini transcription land wherever Gemini saves them. When you want to incorporate one:

1. Drop the transcript (.txt or .md) into `Recordings/Transcripts/`
2. Tell Paul: *"Process the Gemini transcript from [meeting name/date]"*
3. Paul cross-references it with the team member's profile and flags anything notable

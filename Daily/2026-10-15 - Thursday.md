### JEV AI — Standard Deviation Math (10,000 Calls/Month)

Updated the JEV AI automation standard deviation tiers and human review volume:

| Tier | Math | Points Off | Equivalent Flags | Monthly Workload |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 Standard Deviation**  | 3.5% MAE + 4.72% Std = **8.22% total error**    | 8.22% × 180 = **14.8 raw points** | ~7-8 flags       | Worst ~16% of calls = 1,600 human reviews (~400/week) |
| **2 Standard Deviations** | 3.5% MAE + (4.72% × 2) = **12.94% total error** | 23.3 raw points                   | ~11-12 flags     | Worst ~5% of calls = 500 human reviews (~125/week)     |
| **3 Standard Deviations** | 3.5% MAE + (4.72% × 3) = **17.66% total error** | 31.8 raw points                   | ~15-16 flags     | Worst ~1% of calls = 100 human reviews (~25/week)     |

**Operational Decision — Lean toward 4.6% error variance (~460 calls/month)**
- Current load: 400 calls/month = 50 per agent per week
- Recommended load: 460 calls/month = **58 per agent per week**
- Each agent picks up +8 calls/week (58 vs 50)
- This lands at ~1 SD above the mean, catching 16% of calls
- Check flags: ~7-8 per review

## 💬 Approaching Cedric

### What works
- **Be technical and specific** — When he asked about the database, your answer (self-hosted vs. IP allowlist) was concrete. He responded to that. Vague answers probably get vague responses or silence.
- **Don't expect fast replies** — ~1 month is normal for him. If something is time-sensitive, escalate through your direct EVP or find another path.
- **Frame things in terms of outcomes, not preferences** — He asked about your connection plan, not whether Supabase was "good." Lead with the technical question and options.

### What to watch for
- **His conflict of interest is real** — He owns the company that would do the in-house build. His push for in-house may not be about technical merit alone. Keep this in mind when evaluating his advice.
- **Slow team = slow everything** — Whatever path you choose, if it requires his team's work, factor in significant delays. Lots of people are unhappy about this — it's not just you.
- **The Supabase path is yours to own** — If you go the Supabase route (self-hosted Streamlit connecting to your own Postgres), you don't need Cedric's team. That's the path with the most control and fastest timeline.

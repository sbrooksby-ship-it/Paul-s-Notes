## 2. New — Backup Payment Method: Adoption ≠ Usage

**Source:** Sherry's cohort submission (email campaigns, Recharge tool)

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Email 1 open rate | 30.82% |
| Email 2 open rate | 24.50% |
| Email 2 click rate | 1.26% |
| Total opt-ins | 360 |
| Successful backup payment transactions | 3 |

**Key finding:** 360 opt-ins but only **3 successful transactions** (0.83% conversion). Adoption ≠ usage.

**What's missing:** No measure of retry success, recovered revenue, or future declines. Recharge has no performance metric available, so monthly email updates (Maggie) are the only monitoring.

**Measurement gap:** Opt-in rate is tracked, but successful transaction rate is not. Without this, the report can't determine if the tool impacts retry success or recovered revenue.

**Deployment decision threshold:** A campaign that generates X opt-ins but few or no successful transactions should not be scaled based on opt-in alone. Add "successful backup payment + recovered revenue" to the quarterly monitoring cadence.

## 3. Cohort Metrics — Correlation vs. Causation Required

**Source (Team-Brain):** Sherry (Director, Division 3) owns cohort metrics. The requirement is that metrics must show **correlation AND causation**, not just correlation.

**Gap:** The only cohort metrics requirement is for Sherry's Division 3 cohort. Rykker's Div 6 cohort projects (VIP box, success story recognition) have no stated measurement targets or pass/fail criteria.

**Fix:** The Div 6 cohort projects need measurement targets defined — what proves the VIP box or success-story recognition actually improves retention/engagement?

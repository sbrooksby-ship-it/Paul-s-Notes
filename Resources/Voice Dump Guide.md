# Voice Dump — Quick Reference

*A 2-minute brain dump after every 1-on-1. Speak naturally. Paul handles the rest.*

---

## What to Cover (in any order)

Don't script it. Just talk. But if you want a mental checklist:

1. **Energy** — How are they feeling? Up, down, flat?
2. **Wins** — What did they crush? What are you proud of?
3. **Blockers** — What's frustrating them? CRM? Software? Process?
4. **Promises** — What did you say you'd do? By when?
5. ** vibes** — Anything off? Anything to watch?

---

## Example Dump

> "I just met with Michael. His energy is a bit low today — seemed distracted. He's annoyed by the CRM lagging again. He crushed the Q3 report though, honestly nailed it. I need to ask IT for a new software license for him by Friday. He's also asking about the promotion timeline — I told him I'd have an answer after next week's review. Overall solid meeting, but the CRM thing is becoming a pattern I should address."

## How to Get the Audio to Paul

| Method | How |
|---|---|
| **Phone memo** | Record → share/file → drag into Hermes chat with Paul |
| **Windows Voice Recorder** | Record → find file in `Documents/Voice Memos` → drag into chat |
| **Any app** | Save as .m4a / .mp3 / .wav → drag into chat |
| **Save to folder** | Drop into `Recordings/Audio/` → tell Paul "process the latest audio" |

Paul transcribes, structures it into a 1-on-1 note, updates the team profile, and extracts your action items. You do nothing else.

---

## What Paul Produces

From your 2-minute ramble, Paul creates:

1. **A structured 1-on-1 note** in `1-on-1s/{Name}/` using the template
2. **An update to the team profile** — wins appended, blockers flagged, promises added
3. **Action items extracted** — anything you said "I need to…" becomes a tracked task
4. **A summary** you can scan in 10 seconds

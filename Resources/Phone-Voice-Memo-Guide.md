# Phone Voice Memo Guide

*The fastest way to record a post-meeting dump: your phone, 2 minutes, done.*

---

## The 30-Second Workflow

1. **Meeting ends**
2. **Open your phone's voice memo app**
3. **Hit record**
4. **Speak for 2 minutes** — your raw impressions
5. **Stop recording**
6. **Send the file to your computer**
7. **Drag it into chat with @paul**
8. **Done** — Paul transcribes and files everything

That's it. No app to install. No setup. Just your phone's built-in recorder.

---

## Sending the File to Your Computer

| Method | How |
|---|---|
| **AirDrop** (Mac → Mac) | Record on phone → AirDrop to desktop → drag to Paul |
| **Email** | Record → share via email to yourself → download attachment → drag to Paul |
| **Google Drive / iCloud / OneDrive** | Record → save to cloud → open on desktop → drag to Paul |
| **USB cable** | Plug phone into computer → copy file → drag to Paul |
| **WhatsApp / Telegram / Signal** | Send to yourself → download on desktop → drag to Paul |
| **QR code / quick transfer apps** | Apps like "Send Anywhere" or "Snapdrop" for quick local transfer |

**Easiest:** If you're on the same ecosystem (Mac+iPhone, Windows+Android with Link to Windows), the transfer is one tap. Otherwise, email-to-self is universal and fast.

## What to Say in 2 Minutes

Don't script it. Just talk. Here's the mental checklist:

1. **Who** — "Just met with Michael"
2. **Energy** — "His energy was solid / low / high today"
3. **Wins** — "He crushed X" / "Progress on Y"
4. **Blockers** — "He's frustrated by Z" / "CRM is lagging again"
5. **Promises** — "I need to do X by Friday" / "I told him I'd follow up on Y"
6. **Vibes** — "Something felt off" / "He seemed distracted" / "Great conversation"

**Example (real, from your intro meeting if you'd done a dump):**

> "Just met with Michael and Brad. First meeting as his manager. Michael's energy is high — he's relieved about the transition, said Aaron was overwhelmed. He's a self-starter, brings solutions not problems. Asked him how he defines success — he said consistency plus quantified output, wants metrics. He's loyal, not going anywhere. Said he's a yes person, always available for projects. No blockers raised — he doesn't naturally complain so I need to probe harder next time. He joked about AI stuff breaking. Brad reiterated the 42-hour cap. I'm waiting on Michael to send me the agent list. We agreed to bi-weekly check-ins. Next time I should ask about those AI tools and what projects he wants to get involved in."

That's 2 minutes. Paul takes it from there.

## Phone App Recommendations

**You already have one.** The built-in voice memo app on iOS or Android is perfect. Don't overthink it.

If you want something with more features:
- **iOS:** Voice Memos (built-in) — simple, syncs to iCloud
- **Android:** Voice Recorder (built-in) — usually saves .m4a or .wav
- **Otter.ai** — records + transcribes in real time (but Gemini is already doing the transcription for your meetings)
- **Dolby On** — free, decent audio quality, filters noise

**For 2-minute dumps, built-in is fine.** The quality just needs to be good enough for transcription, and phone mics in 2026 are plenty good for that.

## When to Use Phone vs. Desktop Recorder

| | **Phone** | **Desktop Recorder** |
|---|---|---|
| **Post-meeting 2-min dump** | ✅ Best — you walk away from the meeting, pull out your phone, done | ✅ Also works — open the HTML file, click record |
| **Full meeting recording** | ❌ Not ideal — phone mic across the room | ✅ Best — desktop recorder captures system audio + mic |
| **Setup required** | None | Open `Recorder.html` in browser once |
| **File gets to Paul** | Email/AirDrop/drag | Auto-downloads to your folder, or drag |

**My recommendation:** Use your phone for post-meeting dumps (zero friction). Use the desktop recorder for full meeting recordings (better audio, system audio capture). Both produce files you drag into chat with Paul.

---

## Pro Tip: Make It a Habit

Tie the voice dump to something you already do:

- **After you close the laptop** — record immediately
- **While walking to the next thing** — record on your phone while walking
- **Standing up after the meeting** — record before you sit back down

The 2 minutes needs to happen while the conversation is fresh. If you wait until later in the day, you'll forget the nuance.

## Scoring Category Focus: Personalized Education

### Current Performance
- **Team average:** 10 out of 25 (40%)
- **Passing threshold:** 80%
- **Gap:** Significant — team is well below passing

### Two Specific Scoring Points Discussed

#### Point 2: Personalized Education 2
> "Use purposeful discovery questions to understand the customer situation, uncover needs and guide the conversation effectively."

- **Current average:** 1.3 out of 5
- **Issue:** Team is reactive — letting customers guide the conversation rather than asking purposeful discovery questions. Information the agent knows often came from the customer providing it, not from the agent asking.
- **What "good" looks like:** Asking discovery questions like "Oh, why only fruits?" or "What brought you to Balance of Nature?" — being curious about the customer's goals, situation, and motivations before jumping to recommendations.

#### Point 5: Personalized Education 5
> "Did the customer identify and educate the customer on the next appropriate steps in their wellness journey and current level of engagement with Balance of Nature?"

- **Issue:** Recommendations tend to feel generic — agents throw in suggestions without tying them to the customer's specific situation
- **What "good" looks like:** Once you've identified the customer's success with the product, tie the next step into that naturally. Example: "We're glad you're having success with the product. A lot of people who've had success also do the Veggies or the whole Health System. Because you've already been enjoying it, this would make sense for you." — more personalized, not just throwing in a recommendation to check a box.

## 💡 Aaron's Contribution — Verification Without Losing ARC

Aaron offered a different solution to the verify-first problem (from his own experience in Block 2, where efficiency was critical):

> Open with: *"It's a great day with Balance of Nature. Can I get your name just to verify I'm in the correct account? Not that I don't trust you — I want to make sure I'm in the correct account and doing the right thing for you."*

- **The reframe:** put the verification on **himself** (making sure he does the right thing) rather than on the customer (proving who they are). Softer, less interrogative.
- **Why it works:** as soon as they give their name, he can take his time finding the account and ask "So how are you doing today? How can I help?" — **building ARC while working in the account.**
- **Why he does it:** when he was less efficient, this gave him room to talk without rushing. And "it's a great day with Balance of Nature, how are you doing today?" rarely gets an answer — customers dive into their issue.
- **Samuel loved it** — called it a really good insight, agreed it lets them build more ARC.

**Compliance side-question resolved in-meeting:** Samuel asked whether someone other than the account owner can make changes. **Rafa's answer:** yes — if they verify an additional piece of info. Requirements: **account name + phone number + one more piece.** A wife or other authorized person can make changes.

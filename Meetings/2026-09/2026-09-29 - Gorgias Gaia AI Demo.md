## 🧠 What Is Gaia?

Gorgias released **Gaia** — an in-product AI assistant — to all accounts in **early access with no current billing**.

- Originally used to draft AI agent guidances (version 1)
- **New update:** pulls across **all account data** for reports, insights, and ad-hoc questions — not just AI agent stuff
- Runs on **top-tier external models** (ChatGPT / Anthropic) connected into Gorgias context
- Think: "Gorgias's own little ChatGPT or Claude that they've connected into our account"

---

## 🔑 Key Features Demoed

### 1. Chat Interface
- Per-team scoping: e.g., "summarize top issues in my queue" scoped to marketing or customer care
- Can ask questions about your team, holes in coverage, what customers are asking, where you're missing with customers
- Real-time thinking shown as it generates
- Example: Cody scoped a query to the **marketing group** — got a breakdown of 29 open tickets with themes (creator influence, UGC pitches, affiliate applications, sponsorship, customer feedback/product safety concerns)

### 2. Routines (Scheduled Recurring Reports)
- Like scheduled tasks / recurring skills in ChatGPT
- Set up once, runs automatically — no need to manually chat every week
- **Routine library examples:**
  - Weekly support digest
  - At-risk conversation radar
  - Weekly automation digest
  - Macro and rule hygiene
- You can build custom routines too
- **Notifications:** can enable desktop notifications when routine completes

### 3. In-Ticket Assistant ("Ask Gaia")
- **Bottom of every ticket:** "Summarize" button — generates summary, marks who generated it + timestamp
- **"Ask Gaia" button** (bottom left) or **keyboard hotkey** — opens Gaia on the right side, synced to the open ticket
- Contextual Q&A: Gaia pulls the open ticket as context automatically — don't have to tell it which ticket/customer to look up
- **Demo: customer history pull** — Kirsha asked about a customer with 9+ orders; Gaia found **21 tickets**, grouped recurring themes (subscription holds/cancellation requests, shipping, payment, account issues), paired tickets by topic, confirmed orders
- Also gives team-level focus recommendations based on the full picture

## ⚠️ Limitations & Access

### What Gaia CANNOT Do
- **Cannot read call recordings** — only agent-entered call notes are available
- Data quality drives output quality: "if the data is good from the team, it should be able to infer from it"
- Currently can only see **current chat context** for in-ticket summaries — not full customer history (unless you specifically ask for it and it finds it)

### Access Scope by Role
- **Managers:** broad data access
- **Agents:** likely limited to their own tickets
- Level of access depends on user role

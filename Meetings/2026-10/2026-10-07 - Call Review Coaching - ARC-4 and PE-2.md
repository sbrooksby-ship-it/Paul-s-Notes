## Observations

**The two coaching points are the same point.** ARC-4 (personal connection) and PE-2 (discovery questions) aren't independent — Sophia said it best:

> "By asking more questions, you show that you're interested in the customer. And then by giving them acknowledgements to their answers, it helps them feel seen and heard, and that opens the door for more ARC. And then more ARC opens the door to bring in those personalized experiences. So it's kind of a little snowball."

**Fix one, and the other moves.** That's the one coaching idea to land with team leads this week.

**Samuel's framing worth repeating to TLs:** efficiency and family aren't opposites — *"you can be quick in doing something, but still be friendly and treat someone as family."* That's the answer to "agents think asking questions slows the call down."

**Rafa's caution on real-time coaching is the sharpest insight in the meeting** — it only works if TLs are trained *and* have floor time. And humans get defensive when corrected in front of others, so TLs need language for it that isn't a correction. Both are staffing/training problems, not tooling problems.
